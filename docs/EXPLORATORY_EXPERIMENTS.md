# Exploratory experiments (not reported in the dissertation)

A log of tool runs carried out while the corpus was being built. None of them
supports a reported result. They are listed so that the analytical path is
transparent and the exports in `data/tool_exports/` can be placed in time.

| Date | Tool | Corpus version | What was run | Output kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jun–Jul 2024 | Voyant Tools 2.6.14 | 15 files | Cirrus word cloud, term frequencies | `data/tool_exports/voyant/screenshots/2024-06-25_*`, `2024-07-02_*` |
| Sep 2024 | Sketch Engine (`american_mobsters`) | 15 files (probable) | Keywords: top 50 lemmas and multi-word terms; *Compare corpora* vs OANC spoken and enTenTen21 | `data/tool_exports/sketchengine/` (rank only) |
| 2024 | AntConc 4 | 15 files, 13,527 tokens | Keyword list vs a ≈ 1.02 M-token reference corpus; pronoun frequencies (*you*, *I*, *he*, *him* vs *them*); KWIC/collocates/5-grams of *beat*, *beef*, *violated* | `data/tool_exports/antconc/` |
| Nov–Dec 2024 | Voyant Tools | 15 files, 12,535 words | Corpus summary; LDA topic model (10 topics × 10 terms) | `data/tool_exports/voyant/` |

The interpretive notes written at the time of these runs are not part of the
deposit.
